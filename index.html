Zaili Hou, Ph.D.🧑‍🚀

I am a Research Scientist at World Centric, developing bio-based, plastic-free packaging materials and water-resistant barrier coatings. I received my Ph.D. in Polymer Science from the University of Connecticut in 2023, advised by Dr. Luyi Sun and Dr. Montgomery T. Shaw.

My research focuses on polymers, nanocomposites, functional coatings, sustainable packaging, and responsive hydrogels. I have coauthored 27 peer-reviewed research papers.
