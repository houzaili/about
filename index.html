Zaili Hou, Ph.D.

Research Scientist | Polymer Science & Materials Engineering

I am a research scientist at World Centric, developing bio-based, plastic-free packaging materials and water-resistant barrier coatings. I received my Ph.D. in Polymer Science from the University of Connecticut in 2023, advised by Dr. Luyi Sun and Dr. Montgomery T. Shaw.

My research spans functional polymers, nanocomposites, sustainable packaging, responsive hydrogels, and multifunctional sensing materials. I have authored or coauthored 27 peer-reviewed research papers.

Research Highlights

Nature-Inspired Nanocomposite Coatings

Developed a rotational coating approach for advanced packaging films, improving the barrier properties of PET films by approximately 70-fold. Applied design of experiments and materials characterization to optimize processing.

Conductive Polymer Hydrogels & 3D Printing

Developed a liquid 3D-printing method for elastic, conductive PEDOT:PSS hydrogels, and studied the effects of reduced graphene oxide and waterborne polyurethane additives.

Responsive Hydrogels & Soft Electronics

Designed tunable PVA hydrogel systems for deformable electroluminescent devices and responsive strain sensors.

Multifunctional Sensors & Photodetectors

Investigated hydrogel–semiconductor photodetectors and flexible sensors for impact energy, UV, and IR detection.

Hybrid Thermoelectric Materials

Developed PVA hydrogels incorporating liquid metals and other additives, studying how composition and polymer-chain orientation influence thermoelectric performance.

Selected Industrial Collaborations

  • ResMed: Surface-energy analysis of nasal-mask materials.
  • Vestergaard: Thermal and microstructural characterization of mosquito-net materials.
